# Phase 10 — Privacy, Legal & Trust

## Goal
Replace placeholder legal pages with a coherent V1 trust layer that matches the local-first image architecture and server-side account/payment boundaries.

## Included
- Privacy Policy
- Terms of Service
- Data & cookies disclosure
- Security & trust page
- Payments & refunds page
- Shared legal footer/navigation

## Product truth rules
- Do not claim that every future feature will be local.
- Do not claim zero data collection when accounts, usage or payments are enabled.
- Do not claim exhaustive metadata removal or formal anonymization.
- Do not claim payment success from the browser callback.
- Do not store original images as part of the ordinary V1 cleaning workflow.

## Before production
A qualified reviewer should finalize the legal entity, jurisdiction, contact details, retention periods, refund window, lawful basis where applicable, and security contact. The published policy must match the deployed implementation.
